15 Futuristic Design Languages

One shared component board — top bar, hero, the signature toggle, controls, app cards and a type/colour spec — rendered in 15 distinct skins. Real Nasalization + Exo 2. Pick the ones that resonate and we refine to perfection.

01Obsidian Glass

Refractive frosted panels over a starless black. Warm cream light through cold glass.
02Liquid Mercury

Reflective chrome that flows. Metaball surfaces, mercury controls, weightless silver.
03Aurora Nebula

Living mesh-gradients drift like polar light across deep space.
04Holographic Foil

Iridescent prism sheen that shifts with every move. Sticker-flip controls.
05Aerospace HUD

Cockpit telemetry. Corner brackets, reticles and phosphor readouts on near-black.
06Engineering Blueprint

Technical drawing as interface. Cyan linework, dimensions, a real breaker switch.
07OLED Monolith

Pure black, one electric line. Maximal type, minimal chrome. Built for OLED.
08Bioluminescent

Abyssal calm with a living glow. Organic forms, jellyfish light, soft blooms.
09Plasma Core

Hot energy on cold dark. Igniting controls, magma gradients, reactor glow.
10Quiet Futurism

Calm, generous, serene. One soft accent and a lot of air. Light and dark, equally at home.
11Carbon Titanium

Machined hardware. Brushed metal, carbon weave, knurled dials, anodized accent.
12Quantum Field

A field of nodes and wires. Data made tangible, snapping into place.
13Synth Horizon

A neon grid runs to the horizon. Restrained synthwave, modern and sharp.
14Paper Lab

Light-mode science. Ink-blue on paper, hairlines and a precise grid.
15Vapor Glass

Airy frosted glass over colour. Floating depth, ultra-soft, visionOS-clean.
